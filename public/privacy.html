Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Timbang works without an account. If you never sign in, everything you enter stays on your phone and the app sends nothing anywhere. This page is about what changes if you choose to sign in.

What is stored when you sign in

  • Your sign-in: your email address. If you use Google, Google gives us your email address and a Google account identifier. We never see your Google password.
  • Your Timbang data, so it can follow you between devices: your profile and targets (age, sex, height, weight, activity, goal), your meal plan and food log, custom foods, workouts and lift history, body-weight entries, steps, and your unit setting.

A workout that is in progress, the colour theme, and other things that only matter on one device are not sent.

Where it is kept and who can see it

The data is kept with Supabase, a hosted database service, in its Singapore region. It is encrypted on its way there and while stored. Access rules in the database limit each row to the account that owns it, so other users of the app cannot read yours.

The person who runs Timbang has technical administrator access to the database, as anyone running a service like this does. It is not looked at, and it is not shared with anyone.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking or analytics, and no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No password: you sign in with Google or a one-time code sent by email.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Target → Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and every row saved in the cloud, right away. The copy on your phone is not touched; clear it by removing the app or your browser's site data.

Signing out keeps your cloud data so you can sign back in later. Only deleting the account removes it.

Health information

Weight, height, food and training are personal. Timbang is a planning tool, not medical advice. If you would rather keep it all private, do not sign in: the app is fully usable without an account.
